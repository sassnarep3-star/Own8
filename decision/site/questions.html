How missing information would be discovered

  • Depth and string order: needs an official depth chart or official participation counts. Both were searched on 2026-11-14 and do not exist on nfl.com (see the injuries page). Until a citable official source appears, the field stays blank.
  • Prices and settlement: needs an operator-native, timestamped source with explicit void/push rules. No official NFL page provides odds, so this must come from the project owner, not from an aggregator.
  • Correlation and joint outcomes: needs per-game player logs aligned to fixed thresholds. Official season totals are not sufficient; the per-game logs must be transcribed with their own source links.
  • Skill definition: needs a written rule, not more data. This is a decision, not a search.
