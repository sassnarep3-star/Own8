Injuries and depth

Official injury availability is real and current. Starting-lineup depth is not published by the NFL — verified, not assumed.

The requested starter/backup tracking cannot be built from official data

No. Verified on 2026-11-14: nfl.com publishes no depth chart (AV-1, AV-2) and no snap/share data (AV-4, AV-5). Only position and roster status (AV-3) plus injury/participation status (AV-6) are official.

Any 2nd/3rd/4th-string claim would have to come from a non-official source. Under this project's own rule (never infer, blank if unconfirmed) those fields MUST stay blank and be flagged, not estimated.

What would unblock it

  • An official, retrievable, timestamped depth chart (team-controlled or league-controlled) with a stable URL -- NOT FOUND as of 2026-11-14.
  • Official per-game participation counts (snaps) -- NOT FOUND in the nfl.com stats taxonomy.
  • Operator-declared inactives list ~90 minutes pre-kickoff -- the only official pre-game availability signal observed; see AV-6 for practice/game status.
