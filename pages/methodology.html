Historical predictionFor each week 3–6, use only earlier weeks. Team PF/PA are shrunk with 4 league-mean pseudo-games; expected team score averages own PF and opponent PA, plus/minus half training home-margin effect.Official source ↗
Benchmark baselinePrior-week league mean margin and mean total. MAE is average absolute prediction error. Improvement = baseline error − model error.Official source ↗
BootstrapSeed 20261104; 4,000 week-block resamples of paired errors. Four test-week clusters are inadequate for reliable inference; intervals are illustrative. No skill p-value is asserted.Official source ↗
Not fully unbiasedThis benchmark was developed after evidence retrieval. Rolling-origin separation prevents same-week leakage, not retrospective model selection. A genuinely untouched forward test is still required.Official source ↗
Signal evaluation planPre-register all trials; nested rolling-season training/tuning, outer holdout seasons and a final prospective season. Fit transforms only on training data; audit availability timestamps.Official source ↗
Multiple testsGroup duplicate strategy families; preregister family-wise or false-discovery correction. Do not choose a lucky variant after viewing holdout returns.Official source ↗
Calibration planAssess Brier/log loss for outcomes, discrete score calibration, quantile coverage, ROI after costs, CLV on matched prices, regime stability and cluster-aware uncertainty. Not implemented without the required data.Official source ↗
