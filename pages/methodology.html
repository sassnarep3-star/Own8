Information cutoffFreeze data availability timestamps before kickoff; do not use season-ending aggregates to predict earlier games.Official source ↗
Line signsPredicted home margin = home score − away score. Home handicap = −margin. At a verified market home handicap h, a home cover occurs when realized margin + h > 0 (0 is a push).Official source ↗
Price and EVUse the operator decimal price d, p(win), p(push): EV per unit = p(win)(d−1) − p(loss). No -110 defaults. Moneyline requires a calibrated distribution, not just a mean.Official source ↗
CorrelationUse aligned bet-event observations, not team means. For two binary events only: P(A∩B)=pa pb + rho sqrt(pa(1−pa)pb(1−pb)), subject to feasible probability bounds. No supplied event estimates, no number.Official source ↗
GeneralizationRolling time splits, lagged features, held-out seasons, calibrated probabilities, comparison to a frozen baseline. Report error and abstention, not only winners.Official source ↗
Multiple testingPre-register all variants and test family. Use week/game-block uncertainty and a pre-specified family-wise adjustment; no cherry-picked best backtest.Official source ↗
Prospective settlementImmutable forecast timestamps, exact operator rules, pushes/voids, prices and units; no settlement without official final outcomes. No forecast ledger currently exists.Official source ↗
Skill uncertaintyBrier=(p−y)^2 and log loss=−log(probability of observed outcome). Reward only a conservative out-of-sample improvement after every evidence gate. No finite test eliminates luck.Official source ↗
