Research boundary

The three numerical estimates use 32 season-to-date team aggregates. Teams share games; these are not 32 independent experiments. No IID uncertainty interval, causality, future skill or leg-dependence claim is made.

Combination research protocol

Align legs by game, player, threshold and pregame timestamp. Model the joint distribution, validate calibration on future games, and compare the offered combo price against conservative net expected value. Pairwise correlations do not identify higher-order dependence. Unknown dependence is never treated as independence.
