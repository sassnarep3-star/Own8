Correlation Matrix (Pearson r)

Computed from verified official NFL data. Pearson r measures linear association between two variables across the observations in the snapshot. Descriptive only — not predictive. Full computation: data/correlations.md (repository, includes the Python script used).

How to read |r| ≥ 0.70 = strong, 0.40–0.69 = moderate, < 0.40 = weak. Positive = move together; negative = move opposite. Player-table rows are flagged UNVERIFIED because Next Gen Stats totals appear low vs. games played in the standings (possible partial data). Betting-market correlations (e.g., same-game parlay legs) are blank — odds are not published by official NFL sources.
