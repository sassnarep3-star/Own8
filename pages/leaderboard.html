Proposed qualification and ranking

Points-only, no entry fee or cash prize. Require all gates, frozen holdout plus a prospective shadow season, at least 200 distinct settled game clusters over 12 weeks, positive lower 95% bound for paired Brier improvement and net quoted-price EV, and Holm-adjusted prespecified test success. These are policy minima, not power guarantees.

Rank by lower confidence bound of paired baseline Brier improvement, then lower ROI confidence bound, then lower clustered drawdown. Share unresolved ties. Gold/Silver/Bronze badges are proposed for the first three qualified ranks only. Never award based on projections, sum weekly ROI percentages, or assume −110 payouts.
