Executive Summary

Own8 NFL Lines — Project Overview

Originate, validate, and track NFL betting-line strategies as a leaderboard competition. Data snapshot: 2026 NFL regular season, fetched from official NFL sources on 2026-11-03.

1. Objective

Build a fully automated, source-verified NFL line-origination system. Strategies are originated in-house across single bets, parlays, and player-prop combos, ranked on a leaderboard, and released only when they pass every validation gate. Correlation between legs is measured and documented. Luck is removed by requiring verified skill inputs before any signal is issued.

6. Leaderboard & Rewards (Framework)

Strategies are ranked by gate-pass rate, documented correlation control, and verified-edge consistency. Rewards for top-performing strategies = allocation priority on the weekly signal list. Only strategies passing all six gates are eligible. No rewards are paid on unverified performance.

7. Limitations & Next Steps

  • Add a verified odds source to clear V4 (edge vs implied line).
  • Add the official NFL injury report to clear V6 (availability).
  • Add historical game-by-game data to clear V2 and V5 (sample size, backtest).
  • Re-fetch standings and player stats weekly; re-run correlations; update this summary.
