RESEARCH MODE
Historical estimates only. These are derived model outputs, not official NFL lines or current recommendations. Model settings are a declared research choice: four pseudo-team-games of shrinkage; home advantage estimated only from prior weeks. No betting probability or return is inferred.

Forecast diagnostic—not a betting leaderboard

League-only baseline uses training mean home margin and training mean total. Sample is one season and four held-out weeks. No hyperparameter search is performed. Improvement in a small MAE sample cannot establish betting skill.
