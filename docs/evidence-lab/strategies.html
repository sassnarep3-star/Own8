Proposed competition contract

  1. Pre-register every attempted variant, selection rule, market, cutoff and stake cap. Reserve an untouched confirmation period; log rejected variants too.
  2. Score only locked, executable quotes and settled outcomes. Track pushes, voids, overtime and book-specific parlay rules. No hindsight substitution.
  3. Require all eight gates to pass before entry. Rank eligible strategies by positive, multiplicity-adjusted lower ROI bound after costs; publish uncertainty and drawdown beside rank.
  4. Proposed non-cash reward: 10,000 × simultaneous lower ROI bound, only after eligibility. Break equal scores by registered strategy ID; never reward raw short-run profit alone.
  5. Use season and week blocks for uncertainty. Repeated monitoring requires a new untouched sample or an anytime-valid protocol; do not repeatedly peek at a fixed-sample p-value.

These rules are our proposed policy, not an existing funded contest or an official NFL scoring system.
