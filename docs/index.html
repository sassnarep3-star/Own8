Reading the numbers

  • Every table row carries a source link to an official site (nfl.com / nextgenstats.nfl.com / kansspelautoriteit.nl).
  • Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are blank and listed on Flagged for Review — never inferred.
  • Model lines are computed from official stats; assumptions (home field 2.5, −110 vig, stake caps) are labeled as assumptions (F-07).
  • Market spreads/totals are sportsbook numbers, not official — captured ones are third-party and unverified (F-05). Verify at a licensed book; in NL check Kansspelwijzer.
