Executive summary

  1. Data integrity. All 121 regular-season games of Weeks 1–8 were transcribed from nfl.com and every one of the 512 standings cells (W, L, T, PCT, PF, PA, NET, HOME, ROAD, DIV, DIV%, CONF, CONF%, STRK and two unlabeled columns) recomputes exactly from the game log. Root files data/scores-2026-week8.json, data/standings-2026-reg-w8.json, data/schedule-2026-week9.json and data/nextgen-passing-2026-week8.json agree with these fresh captures.
  2. Leaderboard: no proven skill yet. 12 pre-registered strategies (spreads, totals, moneylines, 2-leg parlays, same-game parlays, QB passing-yard props) were tested walk-forward with no look-ahead. 0 passed all eight gates. The provisional leader, S1 (Ridge margin ratings + home edge (lambda by LOO-CV)), cuts error by 1.7% versus a no-information baseline (raw one-sided p = 0.0265), but its bootstrap 95% interval includes zero and the multiplicity-adjusted p is 0.32. Naive "season point differential" ratings are 23% worse than the baseline, which shows how rewarding hot records means betting on luck.
  3. Luck dominates records. After about 7.6 games, 60% of the variance in team win% is what coin-flip noise alone would produce. 71 of 121 games were decided by 8 points or fewer. True-talent spread is about ±15 win-percentage points (1 SD).
  4. Correlations that matter for combos. These survive Holm correction: QB passing yards vs game total r = +0.44; opposing QBs' yards r = +0.50; QB yards vs own points r = +0.40; QB attempts vs own margin r = -0.20. So "both QBs over" is worth about +200 fair, not +300. Same-game side vs total (r = -0.24, raw p = 0.026) does not survive correction. Different games in the same week behave as independent (ICC ≈ 0), so cross-game parlays can multiply legs.
  5. Week 9 fair lines (no vig, no market input). Largest fair spreads: AZ @ SEA: SEA -5.0, JAX @ BAL: JAX -4.9, LV @ SF: SF -4.0. These lines are unvalidated estimates and do not see injuries or QB changes. First committed in 5eed6d6 2026-11-04T20:29:48+00:00, before the Thursday kickoff.
  6. Market-edge betting cannot be verified from official sources. No official NFL page publishes sportsbook prices, so "beat the book" strategies (including the root dashboard's W9 candidates) are listed but not scored (gate G8).
How to read this site. Every table row links to the official page(s) behind it. Values that could not be confirmed are left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Nothing here is a betting recommendation: a strategy earns a medal only after passing every gate, and none has yet.
