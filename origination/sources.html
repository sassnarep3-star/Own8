Method

  • S1 margin ratings: home margin = rhome − raway + h (h = 0 at neutral sites), fit by ridge regression. λ is chosen each week by exact leave-one-out CV over {0.5 … 80}. Win probability = Φ(margin / σLOO).
  • S2/S4 offence–defence model: points = μ + offteam + defopp ± s/2, ridge with LOO-CV. Gives both margin and total.
  • S3/S5 naive averages: unshrunk season point differential and PF/PA per game, included to show the cost of chasing records.
  • Parlays (S9): disjoint same-week pairs, legs priced independently. Same-game parlay (S10): bivariate normal with margin–total residual correlation from prior weeks.
  • Props (S11/S12): empirical-Bayes shrinkage of QB passing yards toward the league mean (method-of-moments k), plus a shrunk opponent adjustment.
  • Inference: paired bootstrap (10,000) for CIs, one-sided sign-flip permutation (10,000) for p-values, Holm across the family, binned calibration error. Seed 20261104.
  • Luck: binomial variance 0.25/games vs observed win% variance; Pythagorean exponent fitted to 2026 data; one-score = margin ≤ 8.
