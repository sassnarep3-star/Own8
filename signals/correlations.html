Why n is printed on every row

A correlation is only as good as its denominator. Where a row is nested inside a game — two quarterbacks, one game — the game is the independent unit and the row count overstates significance. Every row below prints both, and quotes the honest one.

The trap

The two coefficients that survive a family correction are the two most tautological pairs in the register: a quarterback's passing yards against his own team's points, and against the game total. Both are large, both are significant, and both would double-count the same information if used to price a combo. Correlation is not edge.
