What this page is, and is not

No market line, spread, total or price is used anywhere. The columns labelled 'market' are blank because no official NFL source publishes them. What this page computes instead is the number a market WOULD have to be for the signal to be worth exactly zero. That is a property of the model and the assumed price, not a quote, and it is the honest version of 'publish the fair line, the market line and the difference'.

The verdict

NO ARM IS PROMOTED. 2 of the 18 arms that placed any bet have a hit-rate interval whose lower bound clears the 52.38% break-even rate: B11 (63.11%, low bound 53.47%), S8 (62.14%, low bound 52.49%). Neither is promotable. B11 is a naive rule that is not a registered strategy whose interval clears by only 1.09 percentage points, out of 18 arms tested (B01 is excluded because it placed none); at that margin a family correction removes it. S8 is a registered strategy whose interval clears by only 0.11 percentage points, out of 18 arms tested (B01 is excluded because it placed none); at that margin a family correction removes it. The point is not that these are bad numbers. It is that a 0.1-to-1.0 point margin over break-even, on about 100 bets, selected from nineteen arms, is indistinguishable from luck, and the repository would be manufacturing a signal by promoting it. What promotion requires is a price and an out-of-sample season, not a slightly higher hit rate.
