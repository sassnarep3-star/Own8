The decision actually facing the operator

There are two live options and the project has oscillated between them for ten sessions. Option one: get a price. One timestamped feed with a named venue and opening and closing numbers. That unblocks every price column, the staking half of the leaderboard, closing-line value, and any real promotion decision. Option two: do not get a price, in which case the honest ceiling is the audited research desk that roughly exists now. What is not a live option is adding a twelfth dashboard.

Corrections to this session's own work, published deliberately

The first draft claimed no arm clears the break-even rate. Two do, and the claim is now computed from the data at build time. The first draft measured replacement depth by matching two Game Books row by row, which invented about twenty replacements because the books order positions differently. The first draft computed the break-even cushion from win probability instead of cover probability. All three are on the flagged page with their evidence.
