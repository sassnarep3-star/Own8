A dedicated review section: proposals ranked by what they unblock, with cost, risk and an open decision on every row.

How to use this section, and what is not allowed here

Each suggestion states what it costs, what it unlocks, and what could go wrong. Nothing is implemented before a decision is recorded, and no suggestion is ranked by how interesting it is. The decision column is blank on every row because the operator is the only actor who can fill it.
