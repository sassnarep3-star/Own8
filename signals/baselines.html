How to read the ladder

Every arm is scored on exactly the same games, in the same walk-forward order, so the only thing that changes is the rule. An arm earns the right to exist only if it beats the do-nothing arms above it. ROI is computed at an ASSUMED -110 because no real price exists; it is a hurdle, not a simulation.

The uncomfortable part

A five-game recency rule (B11) beats a coin flip at p = 0.005 on the moneyline, and the registered ridge strategies do no better. If the ladder cannot separate a well-built model from a simple rule on 104 games, the ladder is measuring noise. That is the finding, and it is published rather than buried.
