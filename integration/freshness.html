Freshness audit

Which surfaces are stale, superseded or current — checked against live official NFL pages.

Method

Each published surface's own 'as of' label was read from its HTML/JSON, then compared with live official NFL pages fetched on 2026-11-14 (standings, Week 9 scores, Week 10 schedule, Week 10 injury report). Status values: CURRENT = surface matches the live official state for the claims it makes; DATED SNAPSHOT = explicitly labelled as a historical snapshot (honest, not an error); STALE = presents older data without a current-state counterweight on the same surface; SUPERSEDED BY FACT = a forward-looking artifact whose event has since resolved.
