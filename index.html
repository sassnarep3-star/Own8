How it works

  • Originate: strategies are built in-house across single bets, 2–3-leg parlays, and player-prop combos.
  • Validate: every strategy runs six gates — official-data completeness, sample size, correlation limit (|r| ≤ 0.45 unless documented), edge vs implied line, backtest pass rate, and availability/injury check.
  • Correlate: leg-to-leg correlation is measured and documented; same-game legs require explicit correlation or are blocked.
  • Leaderboard: strategies are ranked by gate-pass rate and verified-edge consistency. Top performers get allocation priority; only gate-passing strategies issue signals.
  • Verify: every table row carries a source link to an official site. Unconfirmed values are left blank and flagged UNVERIFIED. Nothing is inferred.
Not gambling or financial advice. This site publishes analytical strategy frameworks and verified public NFL data only.
