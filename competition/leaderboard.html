From forecast to audited result

  1. Freeze and publishHash the model version, input cutoff, exact contract and explicit research stake; obtain a server-timestamped publication receipt.
  2. Validate every ticket gateUnknown or CONDITIONAL means HOLD. Exact operator prices, final participation and rules must be evidenced.
  3. Grade the official outcomeAppend settlements and score-correction revisions. Never rewrite an entry or count two revisions as two returns.
  4. Evaluate at the frozen lookOne registered final decision, all trial variants counted, no significance peeking.

Why a winning week earns no rank

Proposed screening requires at least 200 settled tickets across 20 NFL season-week clusters; two frozen holdouts; positive simultaneous net-ROI lower limit; Holm-adjusted null rejection; calibration improvement, matched CLV support and exposure checks.

Even passing that protocol does not eliminate randomness or guarantee future profit. The minimum counts are not proof of sufficient statistical power.
