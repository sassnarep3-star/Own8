What can be concluded

The checked snapshot describes an association between scoring and records. Shared opponents, exposure and the fact that points help determine wins limit inference. Neither a high r nor a small p-value establishes out-of-sample profitability.

What a combo needs

Matched player/game identities; actual pregame thresholds; paired win/push/loss indicators; enough distinct weeks; role/era checks; and a calibrated full joint distribution when more than two outcomes are involved.
