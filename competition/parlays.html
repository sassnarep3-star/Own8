Two binary legs only

If the same two win indicators and their correlation are actually measured, their joint win probability is:

p(A and B) = pA · pB + ρ · √[pA(1−pA)pB(1−pB)] max(0, pA+pB−1) ≤ p(A and B) ≤ min(pA,pB)

These are identities with valid binary marginals. They do not supply a missing ρ, handle pushes, or identify a three-leg joint distribution.

Settlement must not be guessed

All known winning legs use the exact locked combined quote. A push, void, DNP or unresolved tie is held until market-specific rules and any exact repriced subset quote are verified.

The engine never multiplies single-leg prices to manufacture an executable same-game payout.
