Protocol, not a guarantee

Confidence limits summarize uncertainty under assumptions; they are not the probability that this particular interval contains the true mean. Resampling whole NFL weeks acknowledges some shared-game exposure, but residual dependence across weeks can still invalidate coverage. All strategy variants and extra evaluation looks must count in the testing family.
