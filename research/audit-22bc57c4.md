# Independent audit — 2026-11-04

**Status: needs review.** This is a targeted audit, not a line-by-line verification of the repository. Existing published recommendations should not be acted upon. File references describe observed repository behavior, not externally verified NFL data. Official links are provided for manual review; they do not validate project assumptions or code.

## Executive summary

The existing GitHub Pages build is reported as built, but several analytical and operational claims are not supported by the implementation. In particular, spread conventions are mixed, unverified market inputs pass an official-source gate, and the purported automated weekly grading does not exist in the inspected pipeline. Reported correlations do not establish bet-leg independence or predictive skill. Randomness cannot be removed from sports outcomes. Do not represent strategies as validated or use the displayed candidates as wagering advice until corrected and tested.
