Validation gates

A conservative research contract: incomplete evidence blocks recommendations, rankings and rewards.

RESEARCH MODE
PASS means PASS. CONDITIONAL, missing, stale, UNKNOWN or NOT_COMPUTABLE is not a pass. One failed or missing required gate prevents eligibility and suppresses ranks and reward points.

Executable guardrails
