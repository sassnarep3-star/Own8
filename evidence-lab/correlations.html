Parlay validation rule

Align legs on the same historical scenarios, thresholds and pregame information. Estimate joint outcomes directly, including shared factors; compare to the actual official offered payout. Do not multiply marginal probabilities unless independence has been justified. Pairwise r cannot specify a three-leg joint distribution.

The engine includes a paired-binary-outcome estimator, Fréchet bounds and tests showing why multiplying marginals fails for correlated legs. Synthetic test cases are not NFL performance evidence. Pushes and voids require book-specific rules and are rejected by this binary estimator.
