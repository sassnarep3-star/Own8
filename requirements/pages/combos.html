The combination mathematics, stated properly

For two binary events A and B with marginal probabilities pA and pB and correlation rho between the underlying indicators, the joint probability is

P(A and B) = pA·pB + rho·sqrt( pA(1−pA)·pB(1−pB) )

Two things follow. First, assuming independence is the same as assuming rho = 0, which is a specific claim that must be justified, and the safe default is not to assume it. Second, rho cannot be guessed from team-level aggregates: two teams' season totals say nothing about whether a particular running back scoring correlates with a particular receiver scoring in the same game. Pairwise rho also does not identify higher-order dependence in a three-leg or four-leg build.
