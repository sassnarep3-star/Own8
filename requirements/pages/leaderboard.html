Rank, ROI, Brier and reward points are blank because no strategy cleared the gates. A null is not a zero score: it means the number cannot be computed honestly.

The rule, so it cannot be changed after the fact

Only strategies that pass every applicable gate appear at all. Among those, ranking is by the lower 95% bound of the skill score, not the point estimate, so luck cannot buy a medal. Every later variant counts toward the same family for multiplicity. Rewards are paper points, nothing else, and no reward is currently awarded.

Why a coin-flip league would beat this board

Over 15 games, a strategy with no skill at all wins 8 or more spread decisions roughly a third of the time. Any board that ranks on raw hit rate over that sample would be ranking noise. The lower-bound rule is the cheapest defence, and even it needs a sample of several hundred before the bound can exclude zero.
