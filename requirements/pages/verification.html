How the reconciliation works

Every club's record, winning percentage, points for, points against, net points, home record and road record is recomputed from the 137-game official log and compared, cell by cell, with the official standings page retrieved on 2026-11-14. A club that misses a single cell is marked unreconciled and excluded from downstream claims. All 32 clubs matched on every recomputable column. That is a strong consistency test between two independent official pages, and it is the reason the Week 10 numbers can be trusted as arithmetic on official inputs.
