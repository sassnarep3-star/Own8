Luck vs skill

How much of the spread in team records is separable from noise at this sample size, and why luck cannot be removed.

Method

Team-level decomposition on the 137-game official log. Observed variance of club win percentage across 32 clubs is compared with the variance a pure coin-flip process would produce at the same number of games, using each club's own observed rate: p*(1-p)/n averaged over clubs. The ratio is the share of the spread that a no-skill process reproduces on its own.

Games per club

Too few to be sure

Point differential is strongly associated with winning, which is close to a tautology and is NOT evidence of predictive skill. The decomposition says roughly 41% of the between-club spread in win percentage is reproducible by a coin-flip process at these sample sizes. That is the number the project must respect before it claims a strategy knows something.

Luck cannot be removed. It can be bounded, and the bound is wide at 8-9 games. The reward rule handles this by ranking on a lower confidence bound instead of a point estimate.
