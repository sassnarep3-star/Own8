Weakly-validated fair numbers for the 13 Week 10 games not yet played, built only from official Weeks 1-9 results.

Method (this project's own arithmetic, not an NFL publication)

B0: home projection = (home PF/G + away PA/G)/2 ; away projection = (away PF/G + away PA/G)/2. Zero venue adjustment by design (no fitted home-field term, and Munich gets none). Win probability = normal CDF with sigma fixed at the pooled 136-game margin SD. This is a benchmark, NOT a calibrated fair price and NOT a betting recommendation.

Why these are not recommendations

The 15 Week 9 lines that this same method produced were graded out of sample and the home number landed on the wrong side in 13 of 15 games. Until a fitted venue term and a much larger test exist, these numbers are a benchmark for future comparison, not a signal to act on. See the Week 9 grading.

Ratings used (sigma_margin 13.1768, sigma_total 14.2035, 137 official games)

Team ratings are points-for and points-against per game recomputed from the official game log, which reconciles to the official standings on 352 of 352 cells. The venue adjustment is exactly zero by design, and the Munich game receives no home-field assumption for the same reason.
