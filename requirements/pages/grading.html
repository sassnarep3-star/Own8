Our home number is this project's fair handicap for the home team, so a positive number means we made the home side the underdog. The graded outcome compares the realised home margin with that number. No sportsbook line is involved anywhere.

What the numbers mean, and what they do not

15 graded games is far too few to separate skill from luck. A 95% normal-approx interval for a 15-game cover rate spans roughly +/-0.25 around the observed rate, so an observed rate anywhere in 0.25-0.75 is indistinguishable from a coin flip. These numbers are a calibration check, not evidence of an edge.

Read every rate below with its n. With n=15 the standard error of a probability near 0.5 is about 0.13.

Read together: the totals were close to a coin flip, the spreads were worse than a coin flip on side selection, and the moneyline probability was better than a coin flip but with 15 observations. There is no basis here for claiming skill, and there is also no basis for claiming the method is worthless. The honest statement is that the method is unproven and n is far too small.

What this grading cannot tell you

'Home number' means the handicap in points the HOME team gives (-number) or receives (+number), derived from S1's fair home margin. This is an OUR-FAIR-NUMBER comparison. There is no closing line in this repository, so no claim about beating a sportsbook price is made or implied.
