Backups & deficiencies

What official sources do and do not publish about who is actually on the field, and which units are short this week.

The finding

The requirement to know when a backup is replacing a starter is only half answerable from official sources: exactly answerable after the game from the Game Book, and not answerable before kickoff at all.

What would close the gap

A pregame starter designation would have to come from the clubs, and nfl.com does not republish one. The Game Book closes the question retroactively and is the recommended pilot (suggestion SG-03).

Nobody is labelled a starter, a backup or a '2nd-stringer' anywhere in this register unless an official source says so. The register lists who is out, who is unresolved and which unit they play in, and stops there.
