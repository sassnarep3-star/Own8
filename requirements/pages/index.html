What this section is for

This is the review layer the project was missing. It answers four questions in order: what was asked for, what is actually known, what is still unknown, and what the next decision should be. Nothing here is a pitch. 2 of the 16 requirements cannot be met at all from official sources, 4 are only partly met, and 10 are met with a check behind them. The ones that cannot be met are marked that way rather than quietly softened, because a dashboard that overstates itself is worse than no dashboard.
