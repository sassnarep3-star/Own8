The rule that makes the gates honest

Missing evidence never counts as a pass, and a conditional gate is not a pass. A strategy that needs a price it does not have is BLOCKED, not PASS-with-a-caveat. This is why the leaderboard is empty: the emptiness is the gate working, not the gate failing.
