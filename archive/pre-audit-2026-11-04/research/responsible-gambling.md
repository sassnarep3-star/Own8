# Responsible Gambling & Legal Notes (user located in the Netherlands)

- This project is a **research/analytics competition**. It does not place real-money bets, and leaderboard rewards are points only.
- If you ever choose to bet with real money: in the **Netherlands**, only use providers holding a licence from the **Kansspelautoriteit (Ksa)**. Verify any provider in the official register first: **Kansspelwijzer — https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/kansspelwijzer** (regulator: https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/).
- **Self-exclusion in NL:** CRUKS (Centraal Register Uitsluiting Kansspelen) blocks you across every Ksa-licensed operator — https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/uitsluitregister-cruks. Minimum exclusion period 6 months.
- Age: gambling in NL is **18+**; many jurisdictions (e.g., most US states) require **21+**. Know your local law before doing anything.
- Never bet money you cannot afford to lose; set deposit limits at your licensed provider; chase no losses.
- US helpline (free, 24/7): **1-800-GAMBLER** — National Council on Problem Gambling: https://www.ncpgambling.org/
- NFL lines shown anywhere in this project that are not from an official NFL source are **sportsbook/aggregator numbers** — dynamic and unverified here (F-05). The NFL itself publishes no odds.
