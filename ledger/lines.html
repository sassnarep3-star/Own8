Week 10, 13 unplayed games. Three separate columns per market: fair (ours), market (the FanDuel line the nfl.com weekly picks article publishes, locked 2 p.m. ET Thursday of the game week - a dated snapshot, never an opening or closing number) and difference = fair − market, computed only when a market value exists.

Order used: side (1) → total (2) → moneyline (3). The moneyline is a transform of the side margin, so it is last. Units of the difference: side = handicap-home points (handicap home = points added to the home team, negative = home favoured; identity handicap home = −home margin); total = points; moneyline = probability points, fair phome − the market's de-vigged phome.
