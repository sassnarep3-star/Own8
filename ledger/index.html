What the ledger can and cannot do today

  • Can: record every official result (137 games), originate fair lines for the 13 unplayed Week 10 games, grade which strategies pass the pre-registered gates, and keep signal and staking apart.
  • Cannot: record any opening or closing price. nfl.com publishes one locked FanDuel line per market (the price store now records it, correcting the earlier "no official line" claim, F-01/X3), but no official page publishes an opening or a closing value. Therefore no ROI, no closing-line value and no stake can be computed (price store, flag LF-01).
