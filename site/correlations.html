How to use these

  • QB + WR yards (r≈0.70–0.76): strongly positively correlated — price same-game parlays with the empirical joint distribution, never by multiplying independent legs.
  • WR1 + WR2 (r≈0.49): positive, not negative — stacking same-team receivers does not diversify.
  • QB + own RB yards (r≈−0.18): negatively correlated (game script) — a rare negative SGP pair.
  • Cover + total (φ≈0): ~independent — standard 2-leg parlay pricing is roughly fair; no edge.
