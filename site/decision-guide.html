Safe path forward

  1. Continue shadow singles only, in the requested order: side, then total, then moneyline. Pre-register each model version and freeze probability, inputs, fair line, contemporaneous market line and residual before kickoff. Grade against official final results; do not call a line profitable or calibrated until the holdout gates clear.
  2. Protect the evidence chain. Keep raw-source values and derived analysis separate; label every capture with source, fetch time, hash, parser version and conflicts. Do not present a missing row as healthy or available.
  3. Keep all strategies off reward rankings until qualification. Use benchmark-relative Brier skill for probabilities and proper-score/error measures appropriate to numeric lines; publish n, uncertainty, multiplicity adjustment, calibration and the stake-independent signal result. Staking/ROI is a later escrow, never a substitute for signal skill.
  4. Hold combination products behind a separate gate. Require directly validated joint probabilities and explicit void/push settlement; no product of marginal probabilities as a substitute.
  5. Do not claim “luck removed.” Use pre-registration, chronological holdouts, controls, confidence bounds and multiplicity correction to limit false discoveries; residual uncertainty remains.

Choices that cannot safely be made automatically

These are not data gaps that code can resolve: (1) whether to license an odds/snap-count feed and its budget/terms; (2) permission to systematically retrieve or republish NFL content; (3) approved private storage, retention and who receives alerts; and (4) whether any reward exists. Until approved, the conservative defaults are official published snapshots only, no open/close or CLV claim, paper research only, no raw-data publication and no reward. These defaults do not imply consent or legal clearance.
