Depth & availability

The depth layer remains blocked. Source routes, proven coverage and the exact evidence needed to unblock it.

Depth order is the input to every backup-replacement feature. Requirement R-23 needs one verified source route for all 32 clubs. No player is assigned a depth rank from jersey number, experience or salary; no image is transcribed by eye.

Blocked: backup-deficiency, offense/defense matchup matrix, replacement depth, per-team starters-out view, announced-starter field and stale-row flags. Shared/tied and empty slots must be preserved. One Game Book page is actual game-day evidence, not a pre-kickoff all-club depth chart or snap-count source.
