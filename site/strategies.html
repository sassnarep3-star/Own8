Rules in force (validation-w10.json, pre-registered)

  • G1 official data only; G2 no leakage (predictions for week w use only weeks before w); G3 sample size (80 games for singles, 40 independent pairs for parlays, 100 player-games for props).
  • G4 skill: paired bootstrap 95% interval of the loss difference against the baseline lies entirely below zero; G5 Holm-adjusted permutation p below 0.05 across the family.
  • G6 stability: positive in both halves of the evaluation window; G7 calibration: expected calibration error at most 0.10; G8 no market data: the strategy can be priced and settled without sportsbook odds.
  • Ranking: by the lower bound of benchmark-relative skill, not by win rate or season-long ROI (the brief rejects ROI ranking).
  • Joint probabilities: parlays and prop combinations are not ranked without a directly validated joint probability and the operator's void and push rules. Both are missing (M1 and M2 not scored).
  • Demotion: a live strategy returns to paper when it fails its gates (ledger/data/demotion-rules.json). No strategy is live.
