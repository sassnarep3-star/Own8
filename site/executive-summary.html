Executive Summary — Own8 NFL Line Origination & Strategy Competition

Date: 2026-11-03 · Session: 28cc78aa · Branch: arena/28cc78aa-own8 Live dashboard: GitHub Pages (see COORDINATION.md for the deployed URL after merge).

Mission

Collect, organize, and analyze NFL data to originate our own betting lines (spreads, totals, moneylines, player props), research signals for singles / parlays / prop combos, determine correlation values, and run the whole thing as a leaderboard competition where each strategy must pass validation gates that remove luck and reward only selectively skill-based edges. Every published value is verified line-by-line against an official or named primary source, with a link on every table row.

What was built

  1. Verified data foundation — 7,548 games (1999–2026) with closing market lines; 479,089 book-level opening/closing odds quotes (2006–2021); 59,989 player-game stat rows (2009–2017); a 2021 modern-era robustness sample; and an official NFL anchor (the NFL's own nfl-football-ops Big Data Bowl data). See docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md.
  2. Line-by-line validation — game results matched 91/91 exactly against the NFL's official published scores; two independent odds mirrors agree within 0.5 pt on 93.4% of spreads / 98.5% of totals (MAD 0.18 / 0.15); player-stats internal consistency 96.9% exact.
  3. Line-origination engine — a transparent walk-forward team-rating model (no leakage) producing spreads, totals, and moneyline probabilities, plus a dual-estimator player-prop line origination (accuracy line + fair 50/50 line).
  4. 13 strategies across single bets, same-game parlays, and player-prop combos, each backtested out-of-sample and scored on a 7-gate leaderboard.
  5. Correlation library — 16 dependency estimates with bootstrap 95% CIs and a 2021 robustness check, the core input for correct parlay/prop-combo pricing.
  6. Clean dashboard site — landing page + category subpages, every row source-linked, plus a clearly labeled Flagged for Review page.

Headline findings

  • One robust skill signal exists: the model beats the opening game total by ~0.80 points (holdout 2016–2021, n=158, bootstrap CI 0.53–1.09, z = 5.68). Closing-line value is outcome-independent, so this is skill, not luck. It earns Silver — the only non-rejected strategy — because profit consistency (G6) is not yet proven (holdout ROI −6.9%).
  • No strategy reached Gold. Naive models do not beat the efficient closing spread/total after vig, and the market's no-vig moneyline is better calibrated than our model (Brier 0.21105 vs 0.2247). This matches the peer-reviewed evidence (Moskowitz 2021) that betting-market edges are small relative to transaction costs.
  • Player props are strongly correlated — the key to combo pricing:
  • QB pass yards ↔ WR1 receiving yards r = 0.695 (2021 check 0.671).
  • QB pass yards ↔ WR2 receiving yards r = 0.756.
  • WR1 ↔ WR2 receiving yards r = 0.492 (positive — stacking same-team receivers does not diversify).
  • QB pass yards ↔ own RB1 rush yards r = −0.178 (game-script tension; a negatively correlated SGP pair).
  • RB1 rush yards ↔ team point margin r = 0.334 (leaders run).
  • Game-level cover ↔ total legs are ~independent (φ = −0.007) — standard 2-leg parlay pricing is roughly fair there; all four cover+total parlay backtests lose the vig (−8% to −16%).
  • Key numbers confirmed: margin 3 in 14.5% of games, margin 7 in 8.9% (2006–2025).
  • Simple prop origination is not a standalone edge (QB +1.7% RMSE skill, CI crosses 0; WR/RB ≤ 0). Its value is as the input to correlation-aware combo pricing.

Luck removal & validation

Every estimate carries a 2,000-sample bootstrap CI; win rates are tested against a binomial null at the posted vig; thresholds are fit on a 2006–2015 train and judged on a 2016+ holdout; season-consistency and drop-best-season checks kill flukes; and CLV is the primary skill metric because it does not depend on whether a bet wins. Seven gates (data integrity, out-of-sample, beats-baseline, significance, luck-removed, robustness, correlation-correctness) gate the reward tiers (Gold/Silver/Bronze/Rejected). Full rules: docs/VALIDATION_GATES.md.

Verification & honesty policy

  • OFFICIAL facts (rules, scoring, season structure) are quoted verbatim from NFL sources (nfl.com, operations.nfl.com, media.nfl.com, the official 2026 rulebook PDF, and the NFL's GitHub org). 21 official facts verified, each linked.
  • Market lines are not published by the NFL, so they are labeled PRIMARY (non-NFL), cross-checked between two independent mirrors, and flagged — never presented as official.
  • Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are left blank and flagged UNVERIFIED. Nothing is inferred or fabricated. Historical market prop lines do not exist in any reachable archive, so prop work is explicitly line origination, not a ROI backtest.

Flagged for review (non-blocking)

  • Market-line provenance (no official NFL source exists for betting lines).
  • 6.6% of spread closes differ >0.5 pt between mirrors (concentrated 2006–2013; 25 games >1.5 pt).
  • 3.1% of legacy player-stat team-games have passing/receiving yardage attribution quirks.
  • Player-prop correlation era caveat (2009–2017 base, 2021 check) and missing market prop lines.
  • The repo started empty; all work is new. Branch-policy note (pinned Arena branch).

Bottom line

The competition did exactly what it should: it found one genuine, luck-free skill signal (opening-total line-beating), quantified the correlation structure needed to price player prop combos correctly, and rejected twelve strategies that could not prove an edge after vig. The deliverable is a verified, reproducible, source-linked foundation and an honest leaderboard — not a set of too-good-to-be-true picks. Next steps to convert the S2 signal and correlations into profit: usage-based prop features, EPA ratings, injury/lineup adjustments, and copula-based same-game-parlay pricing with line-shopping.
