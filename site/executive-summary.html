Executive summary

Status on 2026-11-17. The official data we can read reconciles. A forecasting edge is not established. The depth and availability layer cannot be built yet, because no official depth-order source is verified.

1. What is verified

  • The 32 official standings reconcile to the repo's 136-game log plus 13 Week 10 finals: 160 of 160 cells match (nfl.com standings).
  • 14 of 14 Week 10 games read as Final on nfl.com (18 Nov). The Monday game (Chargers at Ravens) has no verified score, so it is not graded and is flagged (F-04).
  • Week 11 has 13 games and 6 byes. Thursday's Colts at Texans venue is Reliant Stadium, Houston (schedule; game centre). Venues for all 13 games were read from the nfl.com game centres on 18 Nov (data-schedule.html).
  • Lions designations: Kerby Joseph, Cade Mays and D.J. Reed are all Out on the Week 10 report (injury report, F-15).

2. What the model does, and how it did

  • The shadow baseline (B0) uses team strength only: points for and against per game, no injuries, no depth, no venue. Its formula reproduces all 13 stored Week 10 values (F-02).
  • Week 10, 12 settled games: spread error 11.12 points (model) vs 11.21 (market). Total error 10.18 vs 8.83; the market is better here. Moneyline Brier 0.173 vs 0.168 (market). Every model-minus-market interval includes zero. Twelve games are not evidence in either direction.
  • Week 11 fair lines were written in commit beca9d2 from the standings snapshot taken before Monday's game. They are provisional, have no market column, and will be re-originated once Monday is verified final (F-23).

4. What blocks the rest

  • Depth order. The Houston depth-chart page returns 404 today. Without an official per-club depth source or official snap counts, no starter or backup can be identified (R-22, R-23).

  • Price history. No official opening or closing series was found. The locked Thursday lines are the only price points, and 12 of the 14 Week 10 market rows are not re-verified (F-12, Q-01).
  • Rights. Whether nfl.com-derived tables can be republished is unread (Q-10).
