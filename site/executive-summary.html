Executive summary

Updated 19 Nov 2026, with source-specific limits. Week 10 results are settled in the feed, but the standings live re-check was partial and the Week 10 shadow-model sample is only 13 games. A forecasting edge is not established. The depth and availability layer cannot be built yet because no all-32 source route is verified.

1. What is verified

  • The Week 10 official results feed contains 150 games: 136 from Weeks 1–9 plus all 14 Week 10 finals. The 19 Nov standings spot-check matched the 24 rows visible in the fetched chunk; the remaining eight were not observed in that chunk. The stored standings snapshot predates the Monday final and its full live reconciliation remains incomplete (NFL standings; dated check.)
  • All 14 Week 10 games are settled in the feed; Monday LAC@BAL (20–23) was confirmed against the official Game Book on 18 Nov. The 19 Nov scores-page spot-check matched nine visible finals; the remaining five stay supported by dated stored evidence, not this partial re-read.
  • Week 11 has 13 games and 6 byes. Thursday's Colts at Texans venue is Reliant Stadium, Houston (schedule; game centre). Venues for the other 12 games are not verified.
  • Lions designations: Kerby Joseph, Cade Mays and D.J. Reed are all Out on the Week 10 report (injury report, F-15).
  • Week 11 injury report read twice (18 and 19 Nov). Between the reads, 22 clubs moved from "No Injuries Reported" to listed rows and 16 game designations were filled, all on the Thursday game (IND at HOU). Only change counts are published (official report; Injuries, F-34).

2. What the model does, and how it did

  • The shadow baseline (B0) uses team strength only: points for and against per game, no injuries, no depth, no venue. Its formula reproduces all 13 stored Week 10 values (F-02).
  • Week 10, 13 graded games (0 pending): spread error 10.30 points (model) vs 10.46 (market). Total error 9.54 vs 8.50; the market is better here. Moneyline Brier 0.171 vs 0.164 (market). Every model-minus-market interval includes zero. 13 games are not evidence in either direction.
  • Re-verified live on 2026-11-19 (session 29145302): the nfl.com standings, Week 10 scores and Week 11 schedule pages were re-read and match the stored captures on every observed row (24 standings rows, 9 Week 10 finals, 6 Week 11 games; see Sources). Unobserved rows were left as stored, not upgraded.
  • Week 11 has a frozen B0-v2 line set registered after the verified Monday final. It carries model probabilities and its inputs; verified market columns remain blank, so no residual or CLV is published. B0-v1 is retained as a separate earlier version.

4. What blocks the rest

  • Depth order. The Houston depth-chart URL returned the NFL's 404 page on 19 Nov. No all-32 route is verified; one page of one Game Book is not a complete depth register (R-22, R-23).
  • Injury history. The repository records two Week 11 reads; the second is a seven-chunk, 253-row transcription across 24 clubs, with 16 Thursday game-status cells. The brief's 254 count remains unreconciled and the large delta may reflect a capture artifact (F-34/F-37). No full Wed-to-kickoff series or outcome validation exists yet.
  • Price history. The official Week 10 picks article publishes FanDuel lines locked Thursday at 2 p.m. ET; this is not an opening/closing series. The price ledger records 39 locked market rows and no opening/closing values. F-12's “12 of 14” refers to the 17 Nov freshness re-read (2 of 14 game rows re-read), not missing locked-price values.
  • Rights and storage. NFL's terms page restricts systematic retrieval and copying absent express prior written consent. Project-specific permission is unresolved; private-store/ is still inside the public Git repository (Q-10, F-31).
