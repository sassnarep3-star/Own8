Strategy Research — NFL Line Origination & Signal Design

Session 28cc78aa · 2026-11-03. All numbers below are DERIVED by scripts/analyze.py from the primary datasets in docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md (reproducible, seed 20261103). Market data is PRIMARY (non-NFL) — the NFL does not publish betting lines — and is flagged as such. Method references are in docs/SOURCES.md (R1–R4).

1. Objective & framing

Originate our own NFL lines (spreads, totals, moneylines, player props), compare them to market lines, and keep only strategies that demonstrate skill independent of luck. The project is run as a leaderboard competition: every strategy is scored and must pass validation gates (docs/VALIDATION_GATES.md) to earn a reward tier.

Two structural facts shape everything: - Closing lines are highly efficient. Moskowitz (2021, R4) finds mispricing in betting markets but returns too small to overcome transaction costs. Our backtests confirm this: naive models do not beat the closing spread/total after vig. - Skill is measured by line-beating, not by wins. Closing-line value (CLV) — getting a better number than the eventual close — is outcome-independent and is the primary skill metric (R1, R4). A strategy can be +EV yet lose money short-run; CLV separates the two.

2. The ratings engine (line origination core)

A walk-forward, points-based team rating produces a model margin and total for every game: - Each team carries a rolling log of its last 8 games (points for / against). - Expected home points = (home offense avg + away defense avg) / 2; away symmetric. - Model margin = exp_home − exp_away + home-field advantage (expanding mean of home margins). - Model total = exp_home + exp_away. - No leakage: ratings use only games strictly before the current game; at each season boundary ratings regress 25% toward the league mean (roster turnover). - Moneyline probability = Φ(model_margin / σ), σ = std of (actual − model) margin fit on the 2006–2015 training window only.

This is deliberately simple and transparent. It is a baseline origination engine, not a claim of state-of-the-art prediction. Its market Brier score (0.2247) is worse than the no-vig market (0.21105) over 2006–2025 — the market is the better forecaster, exactly as the efficiency literature predicts.

Implications for parlay / prop-combo pricing - Same-game player props are strongly correlated. QB+WR1 yards r≈0.70 means an "independent-legs" parlay price badly misstates the joint probability. Price these with the empirical joint distribution (or a copula calibrated to r), not by multiplying legs. - WR1+WR2 is positively correlated (r≈0.49), contrary to the "target competition" intuition. Stacking two receivers on the same offense is less diversifying than it looks. - QB yards + own RB yards is negatively correlated (r≈−0.18) — a rare negatively-correlated SGP pair that books may over-price. - Game-level cover+total legs are essentially independent (φ≈0), so standard 2-leg parlay pricing is roughly fair there; our P1–P4 backtests lose the vig, confirming no edge.

5. Player-prop line origination (X1–X3)

No historical market prop lines are publicly archived in any reachable primary source, so props are evaluated as line origination, not ROI backtests (flagged). Dual estimator, walk-forward within season (min 2 prior games): - Accuracy line = expanding mean of prior games → RMSE vs naive baselines (last game, position mean). - Fair 50/50 line = expanding median of prior games → over-hit rate should be ≈50%.

Results: QB expanding mean beats the position-mean baseline by 1.7% RMSE (CI −0.2–3.6, not significant); WR1 and RB1 naive estimators do not beat baselines. A rolling-4-median variant was tested and rejected (worse RMSE than expanding mean at every position). The over-hit rate at the fair line is 47.7–48.8% (slightly under 50% because yards are right-skewed and the within-season median lags early-season usage). Conclusion: simple univariate prop origination is not a standalone edge; its value is as the input to the correlation-aware combo pricing in §4. Better prop lines need usage/role features (snap share, targets, game script) — listed as future work, not fabricated.

6. Removing luck (method)

  • CLV as the skill metric (§3): line-beating is independent of whether the bet wins.
  • Bootstrap CIs (2,000 resamples) on every ROI, CLV, correlation, and skill estimate.
  • Binomial luck null: win rate tested against the break-even probability at the posted vig (e.g., 52.38% at −110); p-value reported per strategy.
  • Out-of-sample protocol: thresholds/σ chosen on 2006–2015 train; evaluated on 2016+ holdout.
  • Season robustness: fraction of seasons with positive edge + drop-best-season edge.
  • Market calibration check: no-vig moneyline deciles vs empirical win rate; market Brier 0.21105 vs model 0.2247 → market better calibrated; favorite-longshot bias visible (longshots implied 16.2% → won 19.1%; big favorites implied 91.7% → won 95.1%) but too small to beat vig.

7. Market context (DERIVED, D1)

  • Key numbers: margin 3 occurs in 14.5% of games, margin 7 in 8.9% (2006–2025) — buying on/off 3 and 7 has measurable value; originate lines aware of these atoms.
  • Scoring environment: league avg total 45.8 (2024), 46.0 (2025); home margin ≈1.9–2.1 pts.
  • Ties exist in the regular season (Rule 16-1-3(d), OFFICIAL F21) — moneyline pushes must be modeled, not ignored.

8. Future work (not fabricated, not counted as results)

Usage-based prop models (snap/target share, game script), EPA-based ratings, injury/lineup adjustments, copula-based SGP pricing, and live line-shopping across books. These are the natural next steps to convert the S2 CLV signal and the correlation structure into profit.

Validation Gates & Leaderboard Rules

Session 28cc78aa · 2026-11-03. Every strategy must pass these gates to earn a reward tier. Gates are computed in scripts/analyze.py::apply_gates and published per-strategy on the site. Flagged irregularities and unverified items do not block publication or merge; they are listed on the Flagged for Review page.

The seven gates

Gate Name Rule (market / parlay strategies) Rule (player-prop origination)
G1 Data integrity All Part-A validation checks pass: official NFL anchor 91/91 exact; independent line cross-check within documented tolerance (≥90% of spreads / ≥95% of totals within 0.5 pt, MAD ≤ 0.5); player-stats internal consistency ≥95% exact with median diff 0; schedule coverage 100%. Same (shared data foundation).
G2 Out-of-sample Walk-forward only; ≥100 holdout bets; thresholds/σ fit on 2006–2015 train, evaluated 2016+. Walk-forward within season by construction (no future games used).
G3 Beats baseline @open: mean holdout CLV > 0. @close/parlay: holdout ROI > 0. Accuracy line RMSE beats best naive baseline (last game / position mean).
G4 Statistical significance Holdout ROI bootstrap 95% CI lower bound > 0, or CLV z ≥ 2. Skill bootstrap 95% CI lower bound > 0.
G5 Luck removed @open: CLV z ≥ 2 (outcome-independent skill). @close/parlay: binomial win-rate p < 0.05 vs break-even-at-vig null. OOS R² > 0 and skill CI > 0 and fair-line over-hit 95% CI covers 50%.
G6 Robustness ≥60% of holdout seasons positive and drop-best-season edge still > 0. 2021 robustness skill > 0.
G7 Correlation correctness Parlays: joint probability estimated empirically with bootstrap CI and |correlation lift| > 1% (pricing must not assume independence). Single bets/props: n/a (auto-pass). n/a (auto-pass).

Why these gates: G1–G2 prevent garbage-in and overfitting; G3–G4 demand a real, significant edge over a sensible baseline; G5 is the explicit luck removal requirement (skill must show up in a metric that does not depend on short-run wins); G6 kills single-season flukes; G7 forces correct dependence modeling for combos.

Leaderboard score (transparent, 0–100)

Market / parlay strategies:

score = 100 × (0.35·CLV_component + 0.25·ROI_component + 0.25·gate_component + 0.15·robustness_component)
  CLV_component        = clamp(CLV_z / 3, 0, 1)            (0 if no CLV measured)
  ROI_component        = clamp(ROI_CI_low / 0.03, 0, 1)    (parlays: clamp(ROI/0.05)×(1 if CI_low>0 else 0.5))
  gate_component       = gates_passed / 7
  robustness_component = fraction of holdout seasons positive

Player-prop origination:

score = 100 × (0.35·skill_component + 0.25·fairness_component + 0.25·gate_component + 0.15·robustness_component)
  skill_component     = clamp(RMSE_skill_pct / 10, 0, 1)
  fairness_component  = 1 − clamp(|over_hit_rate − 0.5| / 0.1, 0, 1)
  gate_component      = gates_passed / 7
  robustness_component= 1 if 2021 skill > 0 else 0
