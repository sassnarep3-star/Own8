The two that matter

F-01 · The repository says prices do not exist. They do.

severity high · ledger/data/price-snapshots.json · 39 markets

All 39 markets in the ledger's price store are blank, and the status string attached to them states that no official source publishes the market. That is false. nfl.com publishes FanDuel moneyline, spread and total for every game of the season, locked weekly. The same game appears in both places.

Why it matters: every gate downstream of that file treats the absence of a price as an unavailability of reality rather than a missing capture. Nine strategies are unranked on that basis alone.

F-02 · Six suggestion registers and six question registers

severity high · governance

Six different files in this repository each present themselves as the place where suggestions are recorded, and six present themselves as the place where questions are recorded. A reader who answers a question in one of them has answered it in one-sixth of the repository. This is not a data bug; it is the reason a decision can appear open forever.
