Own lines vs market

Fair line, market line and difference as three separate columns, in one stated convention, with the head-to-head result.

How to read the columns

Three columns, never merged: our fair line, the market's line, and the difference. The sign convention is stated once and used everywhere: margin is home minus away, and the handicap is the negative of the margin, so a negative handicap means the home team is favoured. An earlier version of this workspace printed the two with opposite signs; that bug is fixed and the convention is now recorded in the data file itself.

Blend weight: how much of our number should go into the price?

The fitted answer is zero, and we are not adopting it

Brier score by weight on our own probability · fitted on Week 9 · n = 15

  • Weight 0.00 → Brier 0.1184 (market alone).
  • Weight 0.50 → Brier 0.1502. Weight 1.00 → Brier 0.1899 (our number alone).
  • The score degrades monotonically. Every extra percent of our number makes the forecast worse on this sample.
  • Decision rule recorded in the data file: Do not adopt a non-zero weight until all four hold: (1) at least 60 priced games scored; (2) the weight is re-fitted walk-forward, i.e. fitted only on weeks before the week it scores; (3) the blended model's lower 95% bootstrap bound on per-bet skill is above 0; (4) that survives Holm across the whole strategy family. A weight fitted on 15 games is a description of 15 games.
What this does not prove

The obvious temptation is to publish 'blend weight 0.0, therefore the market is right'. That conclusion is not supported by 15 games either. The correct statement is narrower: on the only week where both numbers and an official final exist, our line was worse, and no weight on it improves the forecast. Sixty priced games would be needed to say anything stronger.
