What would change our mind

Three results that would overturn the conclusion above

  • Sixty or more priced games in which our line's mean absolute error is below the market's, with the paired interval excluding zero in our favour.
  • A strategy whose lower 95% bound on per-bet skill is above zero after Holm across the whole family, i.e. not a single lucky week.
  • A closing price that our model beats systematically. That test is currently impossible because no closing price is captured.
