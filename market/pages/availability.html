Injuries and replacement depth

345 official injury rows, six confirmed quarterback replacements, and a precise account of why pregame depth order cannot be obtained.

The short answer

A pregame 1st/2nd/3rd-string replacement signal is NOT obtainable from official sources at any price. A postgame replacement FACTS table is obtainable and is published below.

What we can deliver today

  • which players are officially OUT / DOUBTFUL / QUESTIONABLE before kickoff, by team and position group (this page, 345 official rows)
  • which named quarterback changes hands, where the club said so publicly (6 cases this week, each with its official URL)
  • after the game: exactly who started on each side of the ball, from the official Game Book PDF

What we cannot deliver, for any amount of effort, from official sources

  • the depth position of a player before the game, for any position, from any official NFL source
  • the identity of the replacement before the club announces it
  • snap counts or participation percentages before the game

The workaround that stays honest

Build the replacement signal from the injury report plus the postgame game book: when a listed starter is ruled Out and the game book shows a different player starting at that position, that is an official, verifiable replacement event. It can be collected from Week 10 onward, and after 6-8 weeks it will give a measured distribution of 'how much worse does the offence/defence perform with the backup', which is the quantity the operator actually wants. Until then any pregame depth weight would be invented.
