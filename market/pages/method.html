Method and limits

What is computed, what is refused, how to reproduce it, and the seven limitations that bound every claim on this site.

What this workspace does

  • Stores official prices verbatim, with the lock time the source itself states and the date of retrieval.
  • Grades every bet against official finals, at a flat 1-unit stake, with the break-even rate recomputed from the prices actually available.
  • Splits signal from staking so that profit cannot stand in for calibration.
  • Ranks by the lower 95% bound of per-bet skill after Holm, and prints ROI beside it without ever ranking on it.
  • Publishes the fair line, the market line and the difference as three columns in one stated sign convention.
  • States, on every correlation, what it cannot be used to price.
  • Tests what each official URL actually returns, and reports the 404s rather than routing around them.

Reproduce everything on this site

Five commands, no dependencies beyond the Python standard library

cd /path/to/Own8
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=market python3 -m scripts.grade
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=market python3 -m scripts.tournament
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 PYTHONPATH=market python3 -m scripts.model
python3 market/scripts/analyze.py
python3 market/scripts/verify.py && python3 market/scripts/crawl.py && python3 market/scripts/decide.py
python3 market/scripts/build_market_site.py

Every seed is fixed at 20261114, so the bootstrap intervals and the permutation tests are identical on every run. verify.py exits non-zero if any check fails.
