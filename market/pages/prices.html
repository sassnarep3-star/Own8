Where prices do and do not come from

The picks article is the only official page that carries a price. It is mutable, so each price stores the date it was retrieved and the lock timestamp the article itself states. nfl.com has no odds hub: /odds and /odds/nfl both return 404, and the game-centre pages carry no price block. Do not look for prices there.
