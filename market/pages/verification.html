What was checked, in plain language

  • The 136-game official log was re-accumulated club by club and compared to today's official standings on every one of 192 cells: W, L, T, PF, PA and net points for 32 clubs.
  • The one Week 10 game already played was added explicitly, so the reconciliation does not silently absorb a missing game.
  • The Rams appear as LA in the standings and LAR in scores. That alias is applied inside the check only, and the alias itself is a recorded check rather than a hidden normalisation.
  • For all 14 Week 10 games, the moneyline favourite and the spread favourite agree. If a transcription had a sign error, this is where it would surface.
  • Every two-way implied probability sums to more than 1, i.e. the book margin is present in the data. A sum below 1 would mean a transcription or arithmetic error.
  • Every graded side and total is re-checked against the sign of its own adjusted margin.
The gap in the verification

One thing this ledger does not cover: an independent second reading of the price page. The checks prove the numbers are internally consistent and consistent with the official finals; they do not prove a second human would read the same numbers off the same page. That is suggestion SG-09.
