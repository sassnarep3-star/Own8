SpreadFit discrete margin distribution M = home − away. Home handicap h is opposite the margin. Price using P(M+h > 0), P(M+h = 0) and actual settlement rules; the mean need not be the fair median.Official source ↗
TotalFit discrete total T = home + away, including overtime/settlement rules; price over/under/push jointly.Official source ↗
MoneylineFit home/away/tie outcome probabilities. With no push, fair decimal odds = 1/p; actual rules may void ties or treat them differently.Official source ↗
Expected net returnp(win) × (decimal odds − 1) − p(loss). Push returns stake. Leave blank without observed prices and calibrated probabilities.Official source ↗
No-vig baselineFor a complete market normalize inverse decimal odds as a baseline, not a uniquely correct fair probability. No assumed −110.Official source ↗
