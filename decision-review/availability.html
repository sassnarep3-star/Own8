Role-tier policy

Own8 schema labels: Starter / 2nd / 3rd / 4th / Unknown. These are not attributed to any current player. Require a dated official depth chart or explicit lineup source for a non-blank tier; otherwise use Unknown. An injury list or roster order is not a replacement map.
